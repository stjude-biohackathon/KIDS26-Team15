This report summarizes the Output of Daedalus pipeline applied to Victoria Knockout Cohort (~27k cells) to evaluate the transcriptional and cellular composition changes resulting from deletion of the Vsx2 super-enhancer. The resulting output is compared with the established scRNA-seq SNAP Workflow to check biological consistency with the results. The resulting Daedalus output is compared against the established scRNA-seq SNAP Workflow to assess biological consistency between the two pipelines.

## Scientific goals

**Background:** *Vsx2* is a transcription factor expressed in retinal progenitor cells (RPCs) and maintained in differentiated bipolar neurons and Müller glia. This complex, cell-type-specific expression pattern is controlled by a super-enhancer (*Vsx2-SE*). This super-enhancer contains all the modules required for expression of Vsx2 in retinal progenitor cells, Müller glia, and bipolar neurons. Bipolar neurons become rods and Müller glia in the Vsx2-SE deletion mutants.

**Goal of the Study:** This is a single-cell RNA-seq study of **mouse retina**, comparing the **wild-type vs. knock-out** of the *Vsx2-SE*. This cohort characterizes the transcriptional changes and alterations in cellular composition as a consequence of deleting this *Vsx2-SE* super-enhancer region in the developing retina, testing whether the defect in RPC proliferation and/or loss of bipolar neurons is reflected in the single-cell data.

**Design:** 4 samples, 2 conditions
- WT: `DYE_2519`, `DYE_2520`
- KO: `DYE_2521`, `DYE_2522`

### What `upstream-analysis` does
Generates a per-sample quality control summary, filters the raw CellRanger output, and produces a Seurat object for each sample.
**Output:** one filtered Seurat object per sample + a QC summary report of the cohort.

### What `integrative-analysis` does
Takes the filtered Seurat objects from `upstream-analysis` and merges them into a single shared embedding, correcting for batch effects so cells can be compared across conditions. Supports three interchangeable integration methods (Seurat anchor-based, Harmony, LIGER).
**Output:** one integrated Seurat object across the full cohort, ready for downstream cell-type annotation and differential expression analysis between wild-type and knock-out.

## `upstream-analysis` ✅

`upstream-analysis` includes the following parts: (1) QC metrics as generated by FastQC, (2) QC metrics as generated by CellRanger count, (3) Seurat QC metrics, (4) Estimating and filtering out ambient mRNA, (5) Estimating and filtering out doublets and (6) Merging filtered data.
